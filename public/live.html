How these timings are measured

These are observed timings, not official agency SLAs or performance measures. First update runs from submission to the first usable “Updated” email. Closure runs from submission to the closure time published by the NYC311 Portal and requires a usable “Closed” email. Closure email delay measures how long that email took to arrive. Median is the 50th percentile; the “90% within” value is the 90th percentile; n is the number of requests that reached and matched that event.
